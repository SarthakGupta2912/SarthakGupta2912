## About

I'm a **Flutter developer** based in India. I enjoy building products that work offline, feel instant, and use AI where it actually helps.

- 📱 I build production apps for **Android, iOS, and Windows**.
- 🧠 I'm exploring **on-device LLMs, RAG, MCP, and computer vision**.
- 🚀 I've shipped **[PathKosh](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Sarthak.pathkosh)** to Google Play and built **[Fridge Roulette](https://github.com/SarthakGupta2912/Fridge-Roulette)**, a 100% on-device AI cooking game.
- 🎯 I'm open to **Flutter, mobile, and AI application developer** roles.

## Featured Projects

### 🍳 [Fridge Roulette](https://github.com/SarthakGupta2912/Fridge-Roulette)

**A 100% on-device AI cooking game for kids.**  
Snap a photo of your fridge — two YOLO detectors and ML Kit OCR find the ingredients, then a Qwen2.5 0.5B LLM writes a kid-safe recipe, all on the phone.

`Flutter` `MediaPipe` `LiteRT` `YOLO` `ML Kit` · [View source →](https://github.com/SarthakGupta2912/Fridge-Roulette)

---

### 🧭 [PathKosh](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Sarthak.pathkosh)

**AI skill trees for any learning goal.**  
PathKosh turns a goal, level, and timeline into a step-by-step roadmap with prerequisites, difficulty, time estimates, and XP. Live on Google Play.

`Flutter` `OpenRouter` `LLMs` `Google Play` · [Get the app →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Sarthak.pathkosh)

---

### 🧾 [LIMS](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Sarthak.lims)

**Offline-first billing and ordering for small shops.**  
SQLite-backed billing, PDF invoices, QR-paired hotspot ordering, and delta sync across Android and Windows.

`Flutter` `SQLite` `Wi-Fi Direct` `Windows` · [Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.Sarthak.lims) · [Source →](https://github.com/SarthakGupta2912/LIMS)
